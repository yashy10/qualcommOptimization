The report focuses on Q3a, the selected quantization-only submission. A short
section records the completed B3 pruning + one-epoch distillation + INT8 result,
its accuracy loss, time constraints, and proposals for follow-up work. B3 timing
is provisional. The main benchmark uses the five-round milestone measurements.

Evidence: results/q3a_perchannel_pct9999_s8s8.json,
results/official_baseline_onnx_fp32.json, results/timing/, results/aihub_profiles/,
results/b3_blocks6-9-16_kd_{fp32,int8}.json, configs/b3_blocks6-9-16_kd.json.
